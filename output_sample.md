## 6. Final Recommendations  

| Recommendation | Action |
|----------------|--------|
| **Book flights ASAP** | Prices rise sharply after 6 weeks. Set a price alert on Google Flights or Skyscanner and secure the Delhi‑Zurich round‑trip now. |
| **Reserve Swiss Travel Pass early** | Purchase online (Swiss Travel System) and have the e‑ticket ready – it saves you queuing at stations. |
| **Confirm mountain tickets** | For Jungfraujoch and Pilatus, pre‑book the specific summit tickets (discounted with the Pass) to guarantee availability, especially in November. |
| **Consider travel insurance** | Essential for medical coverage abroad and any flight changes. |
| **Pack smart** | November weather is chilly (5‑15 °C in cities, colder on mountain summits). Bring layered clothing, a waterproof jacket, good walking shoes, and a universal power adapter (Swiss sockets are Type C/F). |
| **Currency** | Exchange a small amount of CHF before departure (≈ ₹90 per CHF) for immediate needs; most places accept cards, but cash is handy for mountain railways and small shops. |
| **Mobile connectivity** | Buy a Swiss SIM (e.g., Swisscom, Sunrise) at the airport – cheap data plans work across the whole country. |
| **Dining tip** | Many restaurants charge a 5‑10 % service charge; tipping 5 % in cash is customary if you’re happy with the service. |
| **Safety** | Switzerland is very safe. Keep your passport and travel documents in a hotel safe; carry a photocopy when out. |
